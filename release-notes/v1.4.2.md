## ⚡ SparkTube v1.4.2

Changes and updates since **v1.4.1**.

### ✨ New
- **Home feed cache** — previously loaded video cards appear instantly from cache while the fresh feed refreshes in the background.
- **Video card menu** — new 3-dot menu on every video card with Play, Favorite, Share and **Download** (opens the quality picker and the usual progress / Downloads flow). Playlist and Library items also get a Remove option.
- **Sleep Timer** — Timer button next to Download in the audio player, and in place of Background play on the video page. Audio/video stops when the countdown ends.
- **Quick Video ⇄ Audio switch** — change view without losing your playback position.
- **New premium app icon** — gradient play mark with a spark accent, plus a themed monochrome (Android 13+) layer.

### 🎮 Player
- Redesigned controls: Previous, large Play/Pause, Next, with a smaller circular background; the top back arrow now shows/hides together with the controls.
- Portrait seek bar sits cleanly below the video; landscape layout is unchanged.
- **Safer seeking** — dragging only seeks from the visible track/thumb, so accidental touches near the bar no longer jump the video. Intentional horizontal swipe-seek and double-tap ±10s still work.
- Brightness / volume swipe gestures are landscape-only; in portrait the hardware volume buttons are used.
- Better quality and audio selection — plays the best qualities/tracks the source offers, and separate high-quality video + audio streams stay in sync on one timeline. (2K/4K or a specific quality is not guaranteed for every video; it depends on the source, your connection and your device.)

### ⬇️ Downloads & Library
- Live progress, percentage, total size, and speed / ETA where available.
- Pause / resume, share, open completed audio files directly.
- Album-art audio library and per-item actions.

### 🔔 Notification & PiP
- Previous / Play-Pause / Next controls in the notification.
- Reduced notification-tap delay.
- Starting another video now closes the previous PiP window; fixed the stuck black floating window.

### 🛠️ Under the hood
- Many new unit tests (seek gate, sleep timer, PiP state, queue navigation, download metrics, feed cache and more).
- Debug-only APK CI workflow added.
