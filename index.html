⚡ SparkTube — anonymous YouTube for Android.

A lightweight YouTube client built with Kotlin & NewPipeExtractor. No Google account. No API keys. No tracking. No live streams.

01 — About

YouTube without the account, the ads, or the noise.

🔒

Fully anonymous by design

No Google account login. No API keys. No analytics or tracking libraries. Your watch history and favorites stay only on your device — nothing is uploaded or synced anywhere.

🌍

Region-aware trending

On first launch, SparkTube asks for your country / region via a searchable list. Trending results are tailored to that region, and you can change it any time later.

🚫

No live streams — ever

Live streams are intentionally filtered out of the home feed, search results, and the player. The experience stays focused purely on on-demand videos.

🎵

Music tab with genre chips

Trending music plus quick genre filters — Pop, Hip-Hop, Bollywood, Bangla, Rock, EDM, Lofi and more. Switch moods without leaving the app.
